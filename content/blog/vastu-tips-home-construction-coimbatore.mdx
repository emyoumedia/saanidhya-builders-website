Vastu Shastra is an ancient Indian science of architecture that harmonises your living space with natural energy forces. Most homeowners in Coimbatore prioritise Vastu compliance when building their dream home.

## What is Vastu Shastra?

Vastu Shastra means the **science of dwelling**. It provides guidelines on orientation, room placement, material selection, and proportions to create spaces that promote health, wealth, and happiness.

## Main Entrance Direction

### Ideal Directions
- **North-East** — Best for prosperity and positive energy
- **North** — Good for wealth and career growth
- **East** — Excellent for health and new beginnings

### What to Avoid
- South-West entrance — considered inauspicious in Vastu
- Direct alignment between front and back door

## Room Placement Guidelines

### Master Bedroom
- **South-West corner** is ideal
- Head of bed should point **South or East**
- Avoid placing master bedroom in the North-East corner

### Kitchen
- **South-East corner** is prescribed (Agni corner)
- Cook should face **East** while cooking
- Keep sink and stove on opposite sides

### Pooja Room
- **North-East corner** is most sacred
- Deity should face **East or West**
- Use white marble or natural stone flooring

### Children's Room
- **West or North-West** direction works best
- Study table should face **North or East**

## Common Vastu Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Correction |
|---|---|
| Toilet in North-East | Move to South-East or North-West |
| Staircase in North-East | Place in South or West |
| Mirror facing the bed | Cover or reposition |
| Clutter under staircase | Keep clean and organised |
| Broken items in home | Remove immediately |

## Vastu for Coimbatore's Climate

Coimbatore's moderate climate aligns beautifully with Vastu principles:

- North-facing windows catch cool breezes from the Nilgiris
- Extended roof overhangs on West reduce afternoon sun
- Open North-East spaces maximise morning sunlight
