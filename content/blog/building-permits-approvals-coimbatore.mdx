Getting the right permits before starting construction is not just a legal requirement — it protects your property's resale value and ensures structural safety.

## Why Building Approvals Are Non-Negotiable

Constructing without approvals can result in:

- Demolition notices from the Corporation
- Inability to sell or mortgage the property
- No electricity or water connection
- Legal penalties and fines

## Regulatory Authorities in Coimbatore

| Area | Authority |
|---|---|
| Coimbatore City limits | CCMC (Coimbatore City Municipal Corporation) |
| District (outside city) | DTCP (Directorate of Town and Country Planning) |
| Special zones | CMDA or SIPCOT |

## Step-by-Step Approval Process

### Step 1: Verify Land Classification
- Check if land is agricultural (patta) or residential
- Agricultural land requires NA conversion before construction
- Verify land use in the Master Plan / Detailed Development Plan

### Step 2: Engage a Licensed Architect
- All plans must be certified by a licensed structural engineer
- Architect must be registered with Tamil Nadu Council of Architecture

### Step 3: Required Documents
- Sale deed and title document
- Patta and chitta (land ownership records)
- Encumbrance certificate (last 30 years)
- Site plan, floor plan, elevation, and section drawings
- Structural stability certificate
- Soil test report

### Step 4: Submit and Pay Fees

**For CCMC areas:**
- Submit via Tamil Nadu e-services portal or in-person
- Pay scrutiny fees (approximately 0.5–1% of construction value)
- Timeline: 30–60 days for standard residential plans

## Setback Requirements in Coimbatore

| Plot Size | Front | Rear | Side |
|---|---|---|---|
| Below 200 sq.m | 3 m | 1.5 m | 1 m |
| 200–500 sq.m | 4.5 m | 2 m | 1.5 m |

| CCMC scrutiny fees | ₹15,000–₹60,000 |
| Structural engineer certificate | ₹10,000–₹25,000 |
| **Total** | **₹60,000–₹1,90,000** |
