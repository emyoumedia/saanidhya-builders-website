The term **turnkey construction** comes from the idea that once the project is complete, the owner simply "turns a key" and the building is ready to use. It is one of the most popular construction models for busy homeowners who want a stress-free building experience.

## What is Turnkey Construction?

Turnkey construction is a **single-contract model** where one contractor is responsible for the entire project — from design and planning to construction, interior finishing, and handover.

Unlike conventional construction where you manage multiple vendors separately, a turnkey contractor handles **everything under one roof**.

## How Turnkey Construction Works

### Phase 1: Design and Planning
- Site visit and soil testing
- Architectural drawing and 3D visualisation
- Structural engineering
- Government approvals and permits
- Detailed cost estimate and timeline

### Phase 2: Construction
- Foundation and structure
- Masonry and roofing
- Plumbing and electrical rough-in
- Tiling and flooring

### Phase 3: Finishing
- Doors, windows, and shutters
- Painting and waterproofing
- Kitchen and bathroom fitting
- Electrical fixtures and switches

### Phase 4: Handover
- Quality inspection and snag-list clearance
- Documentation and warranties
- Move-in ready delivery

## Turnkey vs Conventional Construction

| Factor | Turnkey | Conventional |
|---|---|---|
| Single point of contact | ✅ Yes | ❌ Multiple vendors |
| Time management | ✅ Contractor manages | ❌ Owner manages |
| Cost certainty | ✅ Fixed contract price | ❌ Can escalate |
| Accountability | ✅ One contractor | ❌ Blame-shifting |
| Stress level | ✅ Low | ❌ High |

## What to Check Before Signing a Turnkey Contract

1. **Detailed scope of work** — what is included and excluded
2. **Material specifications** — brands and grades of cement, steel, tiles
3. **Payment milestones** — never pay more than 20% upfront
4. **Penalty clauses** — for project delays
5. **Warranty terms** — structural (5 years minimum), workmanship (1 year)
